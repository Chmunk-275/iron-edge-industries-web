Who We Are

Dedicated to honest, quality workmanship

Iron Edge Industries was established in 2024 by Michael Rempel, a certified journeyman heavy duty mechanic with years of experience in the oil patch, forestry, and transportation industries. Through extensive Cummins specific training and hands-on experience in the industry, he founded Iron Edge Industries on a simple belief: heavy equipment deserves expert attention.

At Iron Edge, we understand what downtime costs — and treat every job with the urgency it deserves, whether it's a quick repair or a major overhaul, we strive for excellence with every unit that enters our shop. Our goal is to get your equipment back in operation as quickly and efficiently as possible, without compromising on quality or safety. Iron Edge Industries is committed to providing exceptional service you can count on.
