Who We Are

Dedicated to Keeping Your Equipment Running

Iron Edge Industries was founded on a simple belief: heavy equipment deserves expert attention. Based out of the West Hill Business Park in Peace River, Alberta, we specialize in heavy duty mechanical work for the oil patch, agriculture, forestry, and transportation industries.

As a certified journeyman heavy duty mechanic with years of field and shop experience, I understand what downtime costs — and I treat every job with the urgency it deserves. Whether it's an engine overhaul or an emergency field repair, the work gets done right the first time.

Iron Edge is a local operation. I know this region, I know the equipment, and I know what the people who work here need. That's not something you get from a big shop three hours away — it's something you build over years of showing up and delivering.
