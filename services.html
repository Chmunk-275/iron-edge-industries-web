Services

What We Offer

Full-spectrum heavy duty mechanical services — shop and field — for Peace River and the surrounding region.

Engine Repair & Rebuild

Expert diesel engine work from tune-ups to complete overhauls. Built to OEM spec and tested before returning to service.

  • Engine diagnostics & compression testing
  • Cylinder head rebuilds & valve grinds
  • Complete engine overhauls & major rebuilds
  • Injector service & fuel system repair
  • Turbocharger replacement & service
  • Cooling system repairs & thermostat replacement

Transmission Service

Transmission rebuilds, repairs, and replacement for both manual and automatic heavy duty transmissions.

  • Automatic & manual transmission rebuilds
  • Clutch replacement & adjustment
  • Torque converter service
  • Transfer case repair
  • Shift linkage & cable repair
  • Fluid & filter service

Hydraulic Systems

Complete hydraulic system service including pumps, motors, cylinders, valves, and custom hose fabrication.

  • Hydraulic pump & motor rebuilds
  • Cylinder reseal & rod repair
  • Custom hose fabrication & fittings
  • Control valve service & replacement
  • System leak diagnosis & repair
  • Fluid analysis & system flushing

Brake Systems

Air and hydraulic brake service for trucks, trailers, and heavy equipment. Safety inspections and full repairs.

  • Air brake inspections & certifications
  • S-cam & disc brake service
  • Brake chamber & slack adjuster replacement
  • Lining & drum replacement
  • Hydraulic brake bleeding & repair
  • ABS diagnostics & sensor replacement

Electrical & Diagnostics

Advanced diagnostic capability for modern heavy duty equipment — fault codes, wiring faults, and ECM-level issues.

  • Computer & fault code diagnostics
  • ECM / TCM programming & calibration
  • Wiring harness repair & replacement
  • Charging system & alternator service
  • Starter motor repair & replacement
  • Sensor diagnosis & replacement

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled PM services to catch small problems before they become expensive failures. Keep your fleet on the road.

  • Engine oil & filter service
  • Fuel, air & hydraulic filter changes
  • Full machine inspections & reports
  • Greasing & lubrication service
  • Belt, hose & accessory inspection
  • Fluid top-up & coolant service

Welding & Fabrication

MIG and stick welding for structural repairs, custom fabrication, and component manufacture for heavy equipment.

  • Structural frame & chassis welding
  • Crack detection & crack repair
  • Custom bracket & mount fabrication
  • Bucket, blade & attachment repair
  • Guard & skid plate fabrication
  • General steel fabrication

Mobile Field Service

When the equipment can't come to us, we come to it. Fully equipped service truck for on-site repairs across the region.

  • On-site breakdowns & emergency repairs
  • Field oil & filter changes
  • Mobile welding & cutting
  • On-location diagnostics
  • Fluid top-up & minor servicing
  • Covering Peace River & surrounding areas
