Services

What We Offer

We offer a full range of repairs and services for your heavy equipment needs.

Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Expert diesel engine work from diagnostics to reliable rebuilds. Built to OEM spec and tested before returning to service.

  • Engine diagnostics & troubleshooting
  • Cylinder head rebuilds & valve seating
  • Complete engine overhauls & major repairs
  • Injector service & fuel system repair
  • Turbocharger replacement & service
  • Cooling system repairs & services

Electrical Diagnostics & Repair

Advanced diagnostic capability for modern heavy duty equipment — fault codes, wiring shorts, and ECM-level issues.

  • Fault codes & computer diagnostics
  • ECM programming & calibration
  • Wiring harness repair & replacement
  • Control module diagnostics & repairs
  • Starter motor & charging system repairs
  • Sensor diagnosis & replacement

Engine Tuning

Complete engine, fuel system, and ECM level tuning.

  • ECM reprogramming & calibration
  • Emissions system tuning & repairs
  • Custom engine parameters & calibrations
  • Engine part upgrades & replacement

General Services & Repairs

.

  • Oil & filter changes
  • Cooling system flush & refill
  • Brake chamber & slack adjuster replacement
  • Brake linings & drum/disk replacements
  • ABS diagnostics & sensor replacement

Mobile Repairs

When the equipment can't come to us, we come to it. Fully equipped service truck for on-site repairs across the region.

  • On-site breakdowns & emergency repairs
  • Field oil & filter changes
  • Mobile welding & cutting
  • On-location diagnostics
  • Fluid top-up & minor servicing

CVIP Facility

Licensed CVIP inspection facility for commercial vehicles operating in Alberta. We perform mandatory annual safety inspections to NSC standards and issue official inspection reports.

  • Full safety inspection per NSC standards
  • Brake system testing & measurement
  • Steering, suspension & frame inspection
  • Lights, signals & safety equipment check
  • Tires, wheels, rims & coupling inspection
  • Official CVIP inspection report issued
